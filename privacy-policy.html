Summary: Suzart Gravador de Tela Simples does not collect, send or sell any of your data. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics tools. Everything you record stays on your own device.

1. Who created the app

The Suzart Gravador de Tela Simples app (the "app") was created by Mateus Suzart Luz, independent developer, who also maintains and updates it. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information on your Android device. If you have any questions, write to suzartmateus01@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

The app records a video of what appears on your phone's screen, together with the device's internal audio (for example, the sound of a game or a video) and/or the microphone audio, depending on your choice. You can start a recording from inside the app, from the persistent notification, or from the Android quick settings panel. Videos are saved in the Movies/Suzart Gravador folder of your device storage.

3. Data collected

None. The app:

  • does not declare the internet permission and therefore cannot send anything to my servers or to third parties;
  • does not require registration, login, e-mail, phone number or any personal information;
  • contains no ads, trackers, or usage analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • does not use location, contacts, camera, photos, messages or advertising identifiers;
  • does not sell, rent or share information with anyone.

5. Permissions requested and why

Permission / featureReason
Screen capture (Android system consent)Android asks for your confirmation for every new recording. Without it, the app cannot record anything. You can decline.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Required to record the device's internal audio (Android requires this permission for any audio capture) and to record your voice through the microphone if you turn it on. If you deny it, the video is recorded without sound.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show the notification with the record, pause, stop, cancel and view-videos buttons, and the "video saved" notice.
Foreground service (screen capture, microphone and special use)To keep the recording running while other apps are on screen and to keep the persistent control notification.
Start with the device (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To restore the persistent notification after the phone restarts or the app is updated, if that option is on.
Videos (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO; on Android 12 or earlier, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)To list, in the "My videos" screen, the recordings made by the app in the Movies/Suzart Gravador folder.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Suzart Gravador de Tela Simples > Permissions. Some features stop working without the corresponding permission.

6. Sensitive content on your screen

A recording captures everything visible on the screen and everything playing in the audio while it is active: messages, notifications, typed passwords, banking details, images and other people's conversations. Pause or stop the recording before opening information you do not want recorded. Once a recording is made, it stays on your device, and it is your responsibility to store it and share it securely.

7. Sharing at your initiative

The app only sends a video somewhere else when you choose "Share" and pick a destination in Android's share sheet. In that case, the video is handed to the app you chose (for example, WhatsApp, Google Drive or e-mail), and any further handling follows that other app's privacy policy. The same applies if you open the video in a third-party player.

8. Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party services. Distribution through Google Play is handled by Google, which may process installation and update data under its own privacy policy. That information is not provided to the app.

9. Android backup

Android may include the app's internal data (only your settings, never the videos) in the automatic backup of your Google account, if you keep device backup turned on. That backup is controlled by Google and by you in Android settings, not by the developer.

10. Retention and deletion of your data

  • Videos: stay on the device until you delete them (in the "My videos" screen or in any gallery or file app). If you use the ✕ (Cancel) button during a recording, the video is deleted automatically.
  • Settings: are erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Note: uninstalling the app does not delete videos already saved in Movies/Suzart Gravador; they remain yours.

Because I do not keep any of your data on servers, there is no data under my custody to export or delete.

11. Security

Settings are kept in the app's private area, and videos are kept in the device storage, protected by Android's own safeguards (screen lock and device encryption, when you enable them). Since nothing leaves the device through the app, there is no data transmission to protect. I recommend keeping your screen lock on and your system up to date.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children. Minors should use the app with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

13. Your rights (LGPD and similar laws)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018), you have the right to confirm that processing exists, access, correct, anonymize, delete, request portability and withdraw consent. Because the app does not process personal data on the developer's servers, you exercise these rights directly on your device. Even so, if you have any questions, contact me at suzartmateus01@gmail.com. The same approach applies to users in other countries, including those covered by laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA: the app does not collect personal data.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy, for example if the app gains new features. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the version in force. If a change significantly alters how data is handled, I will announce it on the app's Google Play page and/or inside the app.
